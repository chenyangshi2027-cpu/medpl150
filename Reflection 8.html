Reflection 8

This was my first time creating an animated GIF, and the experience was both engaging and surprisingly eye-opening. In everyday life, I mostly use GIFs that are already made, whether they come from social media or messaging apps. Because of that, I never really thought about the amount of work behind them. Through this project, I realized that making a GIF is not as simple as it looks, and being able to create one myself felt both new and rewarding.

Working in Photoshop came with its challenges. I quickly learned that if I wanted my animation to feel smooth, I needed to build more frames. Adding these frames took time, but it also helped me understand how motion is constructed step by step. This process made me pay attention to small movements and details I would normally overlook when viewing a finished GIF. It felt like I was learning to see animation in a more thoughtful way.

One of the most useful tools I discovered was Puppet Warp. Before this assignment, I didn’t know Photoshop had such a flexible feature for animation. Puppet Warp allowed me to move specific areas while keeping other parts stable, which gave me much more control over the final result. It also made experimenting a lot more enjoyable and helped me understand how digital movement can be shaped intentionally.

Overall, creating this GIF taught me that animation is not just a technical task—it’s a creative process. And now, I feel more confident exploring that process on my own.
