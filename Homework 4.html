In the first photomontage, I explored the concept of ecological issues while also expressing the subconscious. As global warming increases and sea levels rise, many animals lose their homes, and the future of humanity is also at risk. The calm ocean, beneath its beauty, becomes a silent warning of future danger. To create this image, I used masking and selection tools to separate the characters and clouds from their original backgrounds, then blended them into the seascape. In my second photomontage, I continued to explore ecological issues and the subconscious by combining natural and surreal elements. The image shows a rainbow water slide in a mountain landscape, with a giant octopus rising from the water below. The gay sliding toward the sea symbolizes the invasion of nature through human entertainment and industrial development, while the octopus represents nature’s resistance to human destruction. To create this composition, I used masking to separate each element, blending to adjust the lighting and texture consistency, and selections to accurately place the slide and octopus within the forest environment.
