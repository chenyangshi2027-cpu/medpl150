Illustrated Environment

original photo final Illustrator version


I chose this environment because it has a simple structure and a unified color palette. This kind of setting allows me to reinterpret the visual elements more clearly, including the relationship between the background, architecture, and shadows. The limited colors create a clean sense of layering, and even with only three tones, each part remains easy to distinguish. The restrained palette also highlights the composition, guiding the viewer’s attention to the shapes and spatial transitions.
